A photo is hard to interpret

Face the camera directly, extend the tongue naturally, and use even neutral light. Avoid filters, strong colored lighting, heavy shadows, extreme flash, and strongly colored food or drink just before capture. Keep the full tongue in focus. If the app asks for a retake, no TCM pattern should be forced from that image.

Photo capture needs a compatible Android phone camera. Existing photos can be selected with the Android system photo picker. A working internet connection is needed for AI analysis.

Analysis and questions

An interpretation begins with visible observations and provisional candidate patterns, then presents one complete multiple-choice questionnaire. Completing that same session refines the result without starting another photo review or another set of questions. The complete session uses one analysis right.

If a request fails, retry from the same session. Check your connection and available rights in the app. If a right appears to have been deducted for an infrastructure failure, contact support with a session ID if available. Do not email your photo or answers.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play. Use the app's restore purchases action if an active plan does not appear after a reinstall or device change. To cancel or change a subscription, visit Google Play subscriptions. Google Play controls the checkout price, renewal, and refund process.

History and account

Analysis history is encrypted and kept on the device. Delete one entry or all history from the app. For the data flow, read the privacy policy. To delete a TCM Tongue Notes account, use the in-app option or the public account deletion page.

Contact

Email d0guk4n@gmail.com with your app version, Android version, what happened, and a non-sensitive error code if shown. Please do not include tongue photos, questionnaire answers, or other health details. For urgent or personal health concerns, contact an appropriate qualified healthcare professional or local emergency service.
