How to request deletion

  1. In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps.
  2. If you cannot use the app, email d0guk4n@gmail.com with the subject “Tongue Notes account deletion request”. Include only the account email or other account identifier available to you; do not send photos, answers, or medical details.

Support may ask for a limited verification step to avoid deleting someone else's account. The request can be started entirely from this page and does not require reinstalling the app.

Email a deletion request

What deletion covers

Deletion removes the Tongue Notes app account and associated backend account identity, active entitlement record, and credit ledger. In-app deletion also offers to erase encrypted history and retained photos from the device. The cloud does not hold a default copy of your analysis history, so an email request cannot erase content stored only on a device you control; use the app's delete-all history action or clear its local data on that device.

Limited records may need to be retained for legal, transaction, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. They will not remain an active Tongue Notes account. If an exception applies to your request, support will explain it.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions to stop future renewal charges. You may do this before requesting deletion, but it is not a barrier to submitting a deletion request.

What happens next

Support will acknowledge the request, perform any necessary ownership check, and confirm when deletion is complete or explain a limited lawful retention exception. For questions, email d0guk4n@gmail.com.
