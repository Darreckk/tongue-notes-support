Purpose and limits

The app describes visible tongue observations and traditional TCM candidate patterns, then uses your answers to produce an educational interpretation. Results may be uncertain, inconclusive, or broadly balanced. They are not a biomedical diagnosis or a clinical assessment.

Health disclaimer: Tongue Notes is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any medical condition. For medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Do not delay appropriate care because of an app result. Concerning or persistent findings should be discussed with a qualified professional. The app does not prescribe medicines, supplements, herbs, acupuncture, or treatment protocols.

Eligibility and proper use

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. Use your own photo or one you have permission to use. Do not submit other people's health information without their authority. Do not attempt to bypass usage-right, payment, or security controls.

AI and image limitations

AI can make mistakes, miss visual details, or produce an interpretation that does not fit you. Color, lighting, camera quality, angle, filters, and staining can change what is visible. A retake recommendation or an inconclusive result is a valid outcome. A TCM pattern is a traditional framework label, not evidence of a biomedical condition.

Plan and purchases

The free tier includes seven complete analyses per calendar month; unused free rights do not carry over.
