Privacy policy

This policy describes how Tongue Notes handles photos, questionnaire answers, local history, account records, and purchases. Analysis requires transmitting sensitive content off your device.

Who this is for

Tongue Notes is an Android application for adults aged 18 and over. It offers educational, wellness-oriented interpretations using traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) concepts. It is not a medical device and does not provide biomedical diagnosis or treatment advice.

Contact for privacy questions: d0guk4n@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Tongue Notes Android app and this support site.

Information processed

Photo and questionnaire

When you choose to analyze a tongue photo, the app handles that image and your selected questionnaire answers. The photo is resized and stripped of embedded metadata before transmission. The photo, questions, answers, and earlier model result may be sent again for the final and optional Deep Analysis.

Analysis and local history

Observations, provisional candidates, questions, answers, final interpretation, and optional Deep result are stored in encrypted local app history. The original prepared image may also remain encrypted on the device so a later Deep Analysis can review it. You can delete an entry or all history in the app.

Account, credits, and purchases

An anonymous or signed-in account identifier, entitlement status, subscription verification details, and a credit ledger are kept by the backend. If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may receive the account identifier and profile information provided by Google. Google Play handles payment and subscription transactions; Tongue Notes receives purchase tokens and status needed to verify entitlements, not full card details.

Support and site access

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are processed to answer you. The website host may process technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and access logs for delivery and security. Please do not email tongue photos or health details.

Where analysis goes

Analysis content is sent over HTTPS from the app to the Tongue Notes backend and then to the OpenAI API. The backend uses the content to complete the requested analysis and is designed not to save photos, answers, or AI interpretation text in its database or application logs. Content may be held briefly in memory while a request is processed. The app stores the resulting history locally on your device.

OpenAI requests are configured with store:false so they are not saved as stored Responses. This does not mean zero provider retention: under OpenAI's standard API controls, abuse-monitoring logs may contain submitted content and are generally retained for up to 30 days, or longer when legally required. Image inputs are also subject to safety scanning and exceptional retention for manual review. Tongue Notes does not represent itself as having OpenAI Zero Data Retention approval.

Firebase services are used for authentication and account or entitlement infrastructure. Google Play provides purchase processing and verification data. These providers may process data in countries other than yours under their own terms and security practices.

Why it is used

  • To assess image quality and produce the educational TCM interpretation you request.
  • To show and manage encrypted local history.
  • To authenticate an account, prevent abuse, verify subscriptions, and maintain accurate credit balances.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

Tongue Notes does not sell your photos, answers, or analysis text, and does not use advertising. It does not upload analysis history to a cloud history service by default.

Retention and deletion

On your device: encrypted history remains until you delete entries, use delete-all, or clear the app's local data. If you keep a photo for later Deep Analysis, it remains with that local entry until deleted.

On the backend: photos, questionnaire content, and analysis text are processed for the request and are not intentionally retained in the backend database. Account identity, subscription state, credit ledger, and limited security records remain while needed to operate your account and resolve transactions. Account deletion removes associated account data, subject to records that must be kept for legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention. If such an exception applies to your request, support will explain it.

At service providers: OpenAI's standard abuse-monitoring retention may apply as described above. Google Play and Firebase retain records according to their own applicable policies. Support email correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

See account deletion for an in-app and web request path. Deleting a Tongue Notes account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Security and choices

Requests use HTTPS. History is encrypted in local app storage. The backend uses authenticated requests, application integrity checks, access controls, and rate limiting. No system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid including personal information or health details in support emails.

You may use the system photo picker instead of granting camera access. Camera access is requested only for in-app capture. You can review, delete one, or delete all local history in the app; request account deletion through the app or this site; and manage your Google Play subscription separately.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date above indicates this version. We will keep the in-app disclosure and Google Play Data safety information aligned with material changes.
